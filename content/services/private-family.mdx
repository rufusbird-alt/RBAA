For families and individuals who have inherited works of art and want an independent opinion on the market. Many inherited works came down with little record of what they are or what they are worth.

Rufus provides updated valuations from a position no auctioneer or dealer can take, since both have a stake in the outcome. The valuation is the starting point for every other decision: whether to keep, insure, divide or sell. Rufus also advises on potentially exempt transfers, the lifetime gifts that can pass works to the next generation, working alongside your tax advisers rather than in their place.

If you decide to sell, Rufus will advise on where and when, and negotiate on your behalf. He acts for you alone, for a transparent fee, and the decision stays with you throughout. For a case study, see [The Thursby-Pelham Collection](/case-studies/thursby-pelham-collection).

*An honest opinion comes before any decision.*
