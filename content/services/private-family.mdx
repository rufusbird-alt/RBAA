For private families and individuals who may have inherited beautiful works of art, and are seeking an independent viewpoint and an unbiased yet experienced opinion on the market.

We can provide updated valuations that take a truly independent point of view, unlike most auctioneers or dealers, who cannot provide truly independent valuation advice. We are not tax advisors but we have experience working with estates and can advise on UK intergenerational asset transfers, sometimes known as potentially exempt transfers.

If you are contemplating a sale, depending on what you own, we would be delighted to advise on where to sell your works, and to negotiate on your behalf. For a case study, see [The Thursby-Pelham Collection](/case-studies/thursby-pelham-collection).
