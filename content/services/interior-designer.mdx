The rooms are coming together. What remains are the great objects, the pieces that become the centre of the room.

Rufus partners with designers on interior projects: you may be considering the commanding portrait above the fireplace, the engravings in the library, the chimneypiece or carpet a room is built around. He sources works across the European tradition, 1500 to 1950, negotiates privately or buys on your behalf at auction, and manages condition, framing, packing, transport and installation. Your client sees every fee, and the terms are agreed before the search begins.

*Designers shape the space. Rufus helps make sure the objects within it will last the century.*
