The rooms are coming together. The architecture, the finishes, the furniture: all of these are your work, and the eye that pulled them together. What remains is the art — and the art must do more than fill a wall. It must hold the room. Become its centre. Lift everything around it.

You may be placing a single commanding portrait above a fireplace, a series of engravings in a library, a full picture hang in a dining room, or an object — a piece of bronze, a cabinet, a mirror — that becomes the piece everyone walks into the room for.

Rufus partners with designers who want the art to stand up to the quality of the interior. He sources works across the European tradition, 1500 to 1950, that match the ambition of the space; he negotiates acquisition privately or at auction; he manages condition, framing, and installation; and where useful he provides the story of the work, so that the client can live with it knowledgeably and speak of it with confidence. The relationship is transparent at every step — your client sees every fee, and the commercial terms are set out before the search begins.

The outcome is a room that is not just decorated but inhabited, and a client who is glad you brought them into the conversation.

*Designers shape the space. We help make sure the art within it will last the century.*
