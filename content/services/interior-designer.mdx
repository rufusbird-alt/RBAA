The rooms are coming together. The architecture, the finishes, the soft furnishings and modern furniture: all of these are your work, and the eye that pulled them together. What remains are the great objects — and art and great objects must do more than fill a room or hold a wall. It must become the soul and the centre. Lift everything around it.

You may be placing a single commanding portrait above a fireplace, a series of engravings in a library, a full picture hang in a dining room, or an object — a piece of bronze, a *pietre dure* cabinet, a mirror — that becomes the piece everyone walks into the room *for*.

Rufus partners with designers who want objects to stand up to the quality of the interior. He sources works across the European tradition, 1500 to 1950, that match the ambition of the space; he negotiates acquisition privately or at auction; he manages condition, framing, transport and installation; and where useful he provides the story of the work, so that the client can live with it knowledgeably and speak of it with confidence. The relationship is transparent at every step — your client sees every fee, and the commercial terms are set out before the search begins.

The outcome is a room that is not just decorated but inhabited, and a client who is glad you brought them into the conversation.

*Designers shape the space. We help make sure the objects within it will last the century.*
