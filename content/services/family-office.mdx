Family offices exist to hold the long view. The art, silver, books and furniture accumulated over generations are part of that view. They are also illiquid, sometimes under-documented, and often a source of disagreement among family members.

Rufus works with principals, trustees and legal counsel on defensible valuations for tax and insurance, room-by-room catalogues that become the authoritative record, and sale strategies that protect price and dignity. Every engagement is unconflicted and carried out for a transparent fee.

*A steward's work is invisible when it is done well. The collection simply becomes easier to hold.*
