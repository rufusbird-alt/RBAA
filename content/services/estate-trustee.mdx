A great house, an inherited collection, a palazzo in <SmallCaps>Venice</SmallCaps> or a manoir in <SmallCaps>Normandy</SmallCaps>. Trustees are often new to the assets and asked to decide about objects they have had little time to examine, while beneficiaries, insurers and tax authorities wait.

Rufus begins by walking the building, room by room. He catalogues what is present, reconstructs the provenance of what has moved, and tests attributions inherited with the property. He values separately for probate, insurance, distribution and sale, because each asks a different question. He arranges loan contracts and coordinates storage, conservation, property liaison, and the quiet, discreet sales that are sometimes the right answer. The work is undertaken at an agreed fee, with full disclosure.

*The first job is to know what is there.*
