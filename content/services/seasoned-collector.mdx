We understand what it feels like to have spent decades building a deeply personal art collection, only to suddenly feel that it lacks coherence — or is perhaps missing that one final piece to make sense of the whole.

Using your own areas of fascination, you may have found the object that you believe could complete the narrative. But it is not for sale. It is languishing in private hands without so much as a whisper that it may come onto the market.

At this point, Rufus can work with you to help define what you are building, advising you with clarity and direction, discreetly tracing the availability of favoured pieces while also sourcing alternative works that would enhance and refine your collection. He can provide detailed buying reports and open private conversations. Should you wish to proceed, he can also assess the condition and authenticity of any chosen piece through careful due diligence, before negotiating purchases on your behalf.

Our contacts, expertise and understanding of the market mix perfectly with your passion for art and desire for a curatorial approach — resulting in a collection that speaks of meaning, coherence and legacy.

*Without counsel, collections become accidental. With counsel, they become intentional.*
