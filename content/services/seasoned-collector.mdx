For the experienced collector who has spent decades building a collection and needs one final piece to complete it. Sometimes that piece is in private hands and not for sale.

Rufus traces its availability discreetly, opens private conversations on your behalf, and sources alternatives that refine the collection. Before anything is bought, he assesses condition and authenticity through careful due diligence, and reports in detail.

*Without counsel, collections become accidental. With counsel, they become intentional.*
