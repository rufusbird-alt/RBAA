For institutions seeking to acquire or deaccession works of art, we understand that some institutions are very complex entities and are subject to all sorts of pressures. We can provide genuinely unconflicted advice on valuations, sales and acquisitions that meet your institution's evolving aims.

If you are seeking a specific work of art which might appeal to a certain visitor demographic, or fulfil a long-standing charitable aim, we are able to assist in finding the right work and the right price (case study in progress, watch this space).
