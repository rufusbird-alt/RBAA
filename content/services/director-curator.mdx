For institutions acquiring or deaccessioning works of art. Boards, funders and shifting priorities all bear on a collection, and the advice has to serve its purpose rather than the pressure of the moment.

Rufus gives unconflicted advice on valuations, sales and acquisitions that meet the institution's evolving aims. A deaccession will be examined later by auditors, the public and successors, so the record matters as much as the price. Where a work would suit a particular audience or fulfil a long-standing charitable aim, Rufus will find it, assess it and negotiate its purchase at the right price. Each stage is documented, so the reasons for a decision can be shown as clearly as the decision itself.
